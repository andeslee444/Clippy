Support Engineer

Cobalt Freight · Remote (US) · Full-time · Apply below

Cobalt Freight runs dispatch and load-tracking software for regional trucking fleets. This role sits on our Support Engineering team — the escalation point when a dispatcher's board goes dark mid-shift and loads stop updating. You'll be reading logs and pairing with on-call engineers as often as you're writing code.

What you'll do

  • Triage and resolve escalated tickets from dispatchers across 200+ fleets
  • Write internal tooling to catch data-sync issues before a dispatcher notices them
  • Rotate through a one-week-in-five on-call schedule with the rest of the team
  • Document root causes so the same failure doesn't reach a customer twice

What we're looking for

  • 3+ years in a support engineering, SRE, or backend role with direct customer exposure
  • Comfort reading unfamiliar codebases under time pressure
  • SQL fluency — most incidents start with a data question

Start date and logistics

This role backfills a teammate transitioning to a different org, so we have a specific start date in mind and it's a hard constraint for this req — not a formality. We ask every candidate to confirm their desired/available start date as part of the application, separate from anything on a resume.

Compensation and benefits

$120,000–$145,000 base plus equity. Full band details on our benefits page. Cobalt Freight is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
