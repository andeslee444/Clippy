Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Fraud

Ledgerline · Hybrid, New York, NY (3 days/week) · Full-time · Apply below

Ledgerline processes card-present and card-not-present payments for roughly 40,000 mid-market merchants. Fraud losses are the single largest controllable cost on our P&L, and this team exists to bring that number down without adding friction that costs merchants real sales. We sit at the intersection of the risk org and the ML platform team, and we ship models directly into the authorization path, where a decision has to be made in under 80 milliseconds.

About the role

As a Staff engineer on Fraud, you will set technical direction for a team of six, own the real-time feature store that our scoring models read from, and be the final technical reviewer on any change that touches the authorization hot path. This is a hands-on staff role — you will write production code most weeks, not just review design docs. You'll also be expected to represent the team in quarterly reviews with the Head of Risk and occasionally with the board's risk committee.

What you'll do

  • Own the real-time feature store (Flink + a custom low-latency KV layer) end to end
  • Set the technical roadmap for fraud model serving, in partnership with the risk science team
  • Chair the weekly incident review for anything that touches the authorization path
  • Interview and mentor senior and staff-level candidates across the broader ML platform org
  • Represent Fraud Engineering in quarterly reviews with executive and board stakeholders

What we're looking for

  • 8+ years in backend or ML infrastructure engineering, with at least 3 at staff level or above
  • Direct, hands-on experience shipping models into a sub-100ms real-time serving path
  • Experience owning a feature store or similarly latency-critical data system in production
  • Track record setting technical direction across multiple teams, not just your own
  • Comfort presenting technical tradeoffs to non-technical executive stakeholders
  • Prior experience specifically in card payments, fraud, or real-time risk scoring is a strong plus

Compensation and benefits

The salary range for this staff-level role is $230,000–$270,000 base, plus a meaningful equity grant and an annual bonus target of 20%. Full details are on our benefits page. Ledgerline is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
