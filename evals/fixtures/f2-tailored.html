Senior Backend Engineer

Meridian Health · Remote (US) · Full-time · Apply below

Meridian Health builds the claims-adjudication platform used by nineteen regional insurers to process eligibility and benefits in real time. We are a 140-person engineering organization spread across four time zones, and we ship weekly to a system that adjudicates roughly 4 million claims a day. This role sits on the Core Ledger team, which owns the append-only event store that every other service in the company reads from.

About the role

You'll design and operate services that sit directly in the critical path of claims processing. Correctness and auditability matter more here than almost anywhere else in the stack: a bug in ledger reconciliation can mean a clinic doesn't get paid on time. You will pair closely with our compliance and data teams, and you'll be expected to reason carefully about idempotency, replay safety, and backward compatibility across schema versions.

What you'll do

  • Own the event-sourced ledger service end to end, including schema evolution and replay tooling
  • Design APIs consumed by both internal services and a handful of external insurer integrations
  • Partner with the compliance team to keep every write auditable and reversible
  • Improve our on-call rotation's tooling — better runbooks, better alerts, fewer 3am pages
  • Mentor two mid-level engineers who recently joined the team

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years building backend systems in a strongly-typed language (we use Go and Kotlin)
  • Direct experience with event sourcing, CQRS, or append-only ledger designs
  • Comfort owning a service through an on-call rotation
  • Clear written communication — most of our design work happens in async docs

Compensation and benefits

The salary range for this role is $165,000–$205,000 depending on level and location, plus equity. Full details are on our benefits page, and our approach to leveling and pay equity is described in our engineering blog.
