Site Reliability Engineer

Palisade Cloud · Remote (US) · Full-time · Apply below

Palisade Cloud runs managed Kubernetes for about 400 mid-market customers. This role joins our six-person SRE team, which owns the control plane that provisions and upgrades every customer cluster. We page roughly once a week across the whole team, which we consider too often, and a chunk of this role is making that number smaller.

What you'll do

  • Own upgrade orchestration across customer clusters, including rollback tooling
  • Reduce alert noise — today about a third of pages turn out to be non-actionable
  • Build better staging environments that actually catch upgrade regressions
  • Take a share of the on-call rotation once ramped up

What we're looking for

  • 4+ years operating Kubernetes in production, ideally as a managed-service provider
  • Comfort with Go — most of our control plane is written in it
  • A track record of turning a noisy on-call rotation into a quiet one

Compensation and benefits

$155,000–$190,000 base plus equity. Full band details on our benefits page. Palisade Cloud is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
