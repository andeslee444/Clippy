Product Designer

Vantable · Onsite, Seattle, WA · Full-time · Apply below

Vantable makes scheduling software for hospital systems. This is our first dedicated Product Designer hire — today design work is split between two PMs and a contractor, and it shows. You'd own design end to end for a product used by roughly 30,000 nurses and schedulers across 60 hospital systems, most of whom are deep in a busy shift when they open our app.

What you'll do

  • Own the design system from scratch — today every screen is bespoke
  • Run research directly with nurses and charge schedulers, often on hospital floors
  • Partner with two PMs and six engineers across web and native mobile
  • Design for genuinely high-stress, low-attention moments — a scheduling error costs a shift

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years of product design, at least 2 owning a 0-to-1 design system
  • Comfort designing for operational, high-stakes software, not just consumer polish
  • Experience running your own research — you won't have a dedicated researcher

Compensation and benefits

$150,000–$185,000 base plus equity. Full band details on our benefits page. Vantable is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
